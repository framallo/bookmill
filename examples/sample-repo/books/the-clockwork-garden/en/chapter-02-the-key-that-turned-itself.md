# The Key That Turned Itself

One spring a single daisy was born with a crooked key. The gardener frowned at it, for a crooked key cannot be wound by hand. She almost pulled the daisy out.

But the crooked key caught the morning breeze and turned a little on its own. Then a little more. By noon the daisy was spinning softly, winding itself, asking nothing of anyone.

The gardener watched for a long time. Then she went down the rows and, very carefully, bent every key just so — not to wind them, but to let the wind do it. She was teaching the garden to keep itself.
